About

About Posey

Posey is a small web app that coaches your exercise form using your camera. It counts your reps, spots common mistakes and tells you how to fix them, out loud.

Your video stays on your device

Posey analyses the camera picture inside your browser. The video is never uploaded, recorded or saved, and no one else can see it.

Privacy, in plain words

  • Camera: frames are processed in this browser tab and thrown away. Nothing from your camera is sent anywhere.
  • No account: there's no sign-up, login or profile.
  • No tracking: Posey has no analytics or advertising scripts and sets no cookies.
  • Downloads: your browser fetches the site files from our host, and, when you start a workout, the pose model and its runtime from Google's model storage and the jsDelivr CDN. Those servers see an ordinary download request, the same as for any website. They never see your video.
  • Saved on your device: only your mute setting and voice choice, in your browser's local storage. Clearing the site's data removes them.
  • Voices: Posey uses your browser's built-in speech voices. Most run on your device, but some browser voices (for example the "Google" voices in Chrome) are online voices, so the short phrases being spoken, like "Go lower", may be sent to that voice service. Pick a local voice if you prefer.
  • Turning it off: the camera stops when you close or leave the page. You can revoke camera access at any time in your browser's site settings.

Safety

Posey gives coaching tips based on common rules of thumb. It isn't medical or physiotherapy advice and it can't see everything a human coach would. Warm up, clear some space, and stop if anything hurts.

Good to know

  • Posey tracks one person at a time.
  • It works best with good light and your whole body in view.
  • There are currently 2 exercises, with more to come.

Give it a try

No account, no install. Just a camera and a bit of space.
