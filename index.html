How it works

Three steps, about ten seconds

  1. Pick an exercise

    Choose a move. Each one shows a stick-man demo of good form.

  2. Step back from the camera

    Allow camera access, then stand where your whole body is in view.

  3. Move, and listen

    Posey counts clean reps out loud and calls out fixes like "Go lower" right away.

FAQ

Questions

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Frames from your camera go straight to the pose model running in this browser tab. Nothing is sent to a server, recorded or saved. See Privacy for the details.

What do I need?

A device with a camera and a recent browser such as Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. The first time you start a workout, the browser downloads the pose model (a few megabytes).

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up. Open the page, pick an exercise and go.

How should I set up my camera?

Put the device at about hip height, 2–3 metres away, with good light and your whole body in frame. For squats stand side-on; for jumping jacks face the camera.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Prop the phone up so it doesn't move, step back until your whole body fits, and turn the volume up to hear the coach.

Why wasn't my rep counted?

Posey only counts full reps. If a rep is too shallow or your arms don't reach the top, it tells you what to fix ("Go lower", "Arms higher") instead of counting it.

Is this medical or physio advice?

No. The rules are common coaching rules of thumb. If something hurts, stop and ask a professional.
